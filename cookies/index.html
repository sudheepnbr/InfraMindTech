Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how InfraMindTech uses cookies and similar technologies on www.inframindtech.com.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

2. How we use cookies

Depending on how the Website is configured, we may use cookies for:

  • Essential operation — basic site functions, security, and navigation
  • Preferences — remembering settings such as theme (for example light/dark mode)
  • Performance / analytics — understanding page visits in aggregate (only if analytics tools are enabled)

3. Types of cookies

  • Session cookies — deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies — remain until they expire or you delete them
  • First-party cookies — set by InfraMindTech
  • Third-party cookies — set by providers we use (for example fonts, analytics, or embedded content), if any

4. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may block or delete cookies, but some site features (such as saved theme preference) may not work as expected.

Common browser help pages:

  • Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari each provide cookie settings under Privacy / History

5. Local storage

Our Website may also use browser local storage for preferences (for example theme). This is similar to cookies and can usually be cleared with your browser data.

6. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our practices or tools change. The “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies: info@inframindtech.com or our Contact page. See also our Privacy Policy.
